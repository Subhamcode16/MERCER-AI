# GLOSSARY-002
# Creative Domain Vocabulary

**Status:** Canonical  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Define the standardized creative vocabulary used by the Creative Intelligence Platform across all Intelligence Domains.

---

# Introduction

GLOSSARY-001 defines the language of the platform.

GLOSSARY-002 defines the language of creative work.

This document establishes the official vocabulary used by the Creative Intelligence Engine when reasoning about products, brands, campaigns, photography, design, marketing, and visual communication.

Every Intelligence Domain extends this vocabulary rather than replacing it.

---

Creative vocabulary is organized into semantic domains rather than alphabetical order.

# Brand Vocabulary

---

## Brand Identity

The long-term personality and recognizable character of a brand.

---

## Brand Positioning

The unique place a brand occupies within the customer's mind.

---

## Brand Voice

The written communication style used by a brand.

---

## Brand Tone

The emotional expression of the brand within a specific communication.

---

## Brand Personality

The collection of human characteristics associated with a brand.

Examples:

- Minimalist
- Premium
- Playful
- Luxury
- Technical
- Heritage

---

# Product Vocabulary

---

## Hero Product

The primary product featured within a campaign.

---

## Supporting Product

Products included to reinforce the primary narrative.

---

## Collection

A related group of products sharing a common theme.

---

## Variant

A different version of the same product.

Examples:

- Color
- Size
- Material
- Finish

---

## Product Story

The narrative explaining why the product exists and why customers should care.

---

# Photography Vocabulary

---

## Hero Shot

The primary visual representing a campaign.

---

## Editorial Photography

Photography focused on storytelling rather than direct selling.

---

## Commercial Photography

Photography intended primarily to support product sales.

---

## Lifestyle Photography

Photography placing products within realistic human environments.

---

## Studio Photography

Controlled photography using artificial lighting and isolated backgrounds.

---

## Detail Shot

A close-up emphasizing craftsmanship, texture, or construction.

---

## Macro Shot

An extreme close-up revealing small product details.

---

# Composition Vocabulary

---

## Rule of Thirds

A composition technique dividing the frame into thirds.

---

## Negative Space

Unused visual space that increases emphasis on the primary subject.

---

## Visual Hierarchy

The arrangement of elements according to importance.

---

## Symmetry

Balanced composition around a central axis.

---

## Asymmetry

Intentional visual imbalance creating dynamic interest.

---

## Leading Lines

Lines directing attention toward the primary subject.

---

## Framing

Using surrounding objects to emphasize the main subject.

---

# Lighting Vocabulary

---

## Soft Light

Diffused illumination producing gentle shadows.

---

## Hard Light

Directional lighting producing strong contrast.

---

## Ambient Light

Natural environmental illumination.

---

## Rim Light

Lighting separating the subject from the background.

---

## Golden Hour

Warm natural lighting shortly after sunrise or before sunset.

---

## High-Key Lighting

Bright, low-contrast lighting style.

---

## Low-Key Lighting

Dark, high-contrast lighting style.

---

---

# Vocabulary Governance

A new creative term may become official only if:

- It represents a distinct concept.
- It improves reasoning.
- It has a precise definition.
- It is reusable across campaigns.
- It integrates naturally with the platform ontology.

# Status

**GLOSSARY-002 is the canonical creative vocabulary of the Creative Intelligence Platform.**

Together with **GLOSSARY-001**, it establishes the semantic foundation upon which all reasoning, memory, knowledge graphs, ontologies, and creative intelligence are built.
