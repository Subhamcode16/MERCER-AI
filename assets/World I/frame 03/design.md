# Purpose

Frame 03 is the first moment where the institution redirects the visitor's attention away from architecture and toward material.

Until this point, the building has been the protagonist.

Now the material becomes the protagonist.

The visitor no longer observes the building.

The visitor begins observing intelligence hidden inside matter.

Desired Feeling

The visitor should unconsciously slow down and begin studying the fabric rather than simply walking through the space.

# Visual Priority

Unlike Frame 02, architecture is no longer the visual subject.

Visual hierarchy should now become:

1. Suspended Linen
2. Light Passing Through Material
3. Visitor
4. Architecture

The eye should naturally travel through the fabric before noticing anything else.

---

# Motion Override

Frame 03 introduces the first meaningful environmental response.

Motion should remain physically believable.

---

## Visitor

The visitor is almost completely still.

Only an imperceptible weight shift or one slow step may occur during the entire scene.

The visitor exists only to provide human scale.

---

## Linen

The linen becomes the protagonist.

Movement should feel incredibly slow.

The cloth should appear heavy and naturally suspended.

Movement should originate only from soft environmental airflow.

Anchor points must never move.

The cloth should never flap.

Instead it should breathe.

---

## Light

Warm afternoon sunlight passes through the translucent linen.

The movement of the fabric causes tiny changes in the projected shadows.

These lighting changes should remain extremely subtle.

---

## Dust

Fine airborne particles become visible inside the shafts of light.

The particles drift naturally.

Never resemble particle effects.

---

## Reflections

The polished floor slowly reacts to:

- cloth movement
- changing light
- atmospheric motion

Reflections should evolve naturally.

No artificial ripple animation.

---

## Trees

Visible foliage outside continues moving almost imperceptibly.

Enough to suggest natural airflow.

Nothing more.

# Scroll Behaviour

The frame remains almost static while the visitor reads.

As scrolling continues:

The camera gently advances.

Foreground linen produces slightly stronger parallax than previous frames.

The suspended material slowly occupies more visual importance.

Only after this visual study completes should the transition toward Frame 04 begin.

---

# Transition Target

Frame 04

The Observation

The transition should reveal only subtle evidence of human creativity hidden beyond the fabric.

Do not reveal the entire atelier.

The viewer should feel:

"There is something beyond these materials."

Curiosity should increase.

Information should remain restrained.

---

# Design Notes

Never increase visual complexity unnecessarily.

The environment remains silent.

The material carries the narrative.

Maintain identical:

- Architecture
- Lighting
- Colour palette
- Lens language
- Material identity

Only the viewer's attention changes.

---

# Success Criteria

The visitor should finish this frame feeling:

"Materials contain intelligence before creativity begins."

Frame 03 establishes the philosophical foundation of the institution.

Everything that follows is built upon this understanding.
