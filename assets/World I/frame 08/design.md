# CTA

Content

ENTER ATELIER

Arrow

→

No secondary action.

No supporting text.

No iconography beyond the arrow.

---

# CTA Position

The CTA is perfectly centered within the viewport.

It visually aligns with the glowing doorway.

The button should feel like it belongs to the architecture rather than floating above it.

The doorway naturally frames the interaction.

---

# CTA Design Language

The button follows Apple's Liquid Glass design philosophy.

Characteristics

- Completely transparent background
- Frosted glass material
- Real-time backdrop blur
- High optical clarity
- Rounded capsule geometry
- Very soft white edge highlight
- Subtle internal refraction
- Thin translucent outline
- No solid fill
- No drop shadow
- No outer glow

The button should feel like a physical piece of glass suspended in space.

Not a web component.

---

# Typography

Typeface

Same editorial type system used throughout the experience.

Letter spacing

Slightly expanded.

Weight

Medium.

Color

Pure white.

Opacity

Approximately 95%.

The typography should remain elegant and understated.

---

# Hover State

Hover interaction should feel physical.

Never flashy.

On hover

- Glass becomes slightly clearer
- Border becomes marginally brighter
- Background blur increases subtly
- Button lifts approximately 2px
- Scale increases to 1.02
- Arrow shifts 6–8px to the right

Animation duration

300–400ms

Spring easing.

---

# Active State

On click

The glass compresses naturally.

Scale

0.98

Duration

120ms

Immediately after release

The camera transition begins.

---

# Motion

The CTA itself remains perfectly still.

No floating.

No breathing.

No idle animation.

The only animation is interaction.

The environment provides all motion.

---

# Camera Relationship

The CTA is screen-space anchored.

It remains perfectly centered while the camera glides forward.

As the camera approaches the doorway during the final seconds:

The CTA gradually scales down slightly.

Approximately

5–8%

Its opacity subtly decreases.

The effect should feel like the viewer is naturally moving through the interface.

---

# Transition

Clicking

ENTER ATELIER

should never trigger a traditional page transition.

Instead

The camera continues its forward movement.

The CTA softly dissolves.

The viewer passes through the glowing doorway.

Only then does the Atelier OS begin to appear.

The landing page should feel like the entrance hall of the product.

Not a separate website.

---

# Audio

On hover

On click

A very soft glass tap.

Almost inaudible.
