# Purpose

Frame 02 marks the visitor's first conscious moment inside the institution.

The visitor has already crossed the threshold.

Nothing new is introduced.

Instead, the environment quietly responds to the visitor's presence.

The institution takes its first breath.

This frame should communicate that this is a living architectural space rather than a static building.

# Motion Override

This frame follows the universal motion rules from the template with the following scene-specific behaviour.

## Visitor

The visitor has already entered the institution.

They remain almost still.

At most, one slow and deliberate step may occur during the entire scene.

The focus is not on walking.

The focus is on presence.

---

## Linen

The suspended linen gently responds to a soft natural airflow.

Movement is extremely subtle.

Anchor points never move.

The fabric should feel heavy, real and calm.

---

## Dust

Tiny dust particles become visible only where sunlight intersects the air.

Particles should drift naturally.

Never resemble floating visual effects.

---

## Reflections

The polished floor slowly reacts to tiny environmental movement.

No animated ripple effects.

Only physically believable reflection changes.

---

## Trees

Leaves outside move almost imperceptibly.

Enough to suggest natural air.

Not enough to become noticeable.

---

## Light

Warm afternoon sunlight remains constant.

Light rays slowly evolve as airborne particles pass through them.

No exposure shifts.

No dramatic lighting changes.

---

# Scroll Behaviour

The frame remains alive while the user reads.

As the user scrolls:

The camera performs a scroll-driven forward push deeper into the space.

Foreground linen begins producing pronounced parallax.

As the camera pushes through the space, the screen smoothly transitions into a **fade to black**.

Only after the screen is fully black does the transition to Frame 03 begin.

The user should never feel that one image disappeared and another appeared.

Instead, they should feel they continued walking through the darkness into the next room.

---

# Transition Target

Frame 03

Matter Awakens

The transition should naturally guide the viewer's attention away from the architecture and toward the suspended material, preparing them for the next chapter where the fabric itself becomes the protagonist.

---

# Design Notes

Do not increase visual complexity.

Do not reveal new architectural spaces.

Do not introduce additional objects.

Maintain the same lighting, composition and visual identity established in Frame 01.

Frame 02 exists to slow the visitor down.

It is a pause before discovery.

---

# Success Criteria

The visitor should finish this frame feeling:

"I've entered a place that observes before it creates."

Only after this emotional state has been established should the experience continue into Frame 03.
