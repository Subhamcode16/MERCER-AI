# Visual Intelligence Roadmap

**Visual Intelligence Platform:** A state-driven creative reasoning runtime that transforms user intent into structured creative decisions and renderer-specific execution plans.

The foundational architecture (Phases 1-4) is officially **Frozen (v1.0)**. 

To scale the platform, the roadmap now formally splits into two parallel tracks:
- **Track A (Runtime):** The user-facing operating system that executes reasoning to generate campaigns.
- **Track B (Knowledge Factory):** The internal research machinery that ingests raw fashion data and populates the platform's knowledge graph.
