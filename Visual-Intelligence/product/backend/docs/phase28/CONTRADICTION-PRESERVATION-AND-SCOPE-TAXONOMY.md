# Phase 28: Contradiction Preservation & Scope Taxonomy

## 1. Contradiction Preservation Protocol
When new campaign results challenge established claims, the system does not average out or suppress the conflict. It creates an explicit `ContradictionRecord` with status `OPEN_DISPUTE`, preserving both the original claim and the new counter-evidence.

## 2. Scope Taxonomy
Every learning object declares its boundary:
- **`GLOBAL`:** Universal principles validated across diverse sectors (e.g. contrast safe zones).
- **`DOMAIN`:** Industry-specific (e.g. Haute Couture vs Fast Fashion).
- **`CLIENT`:** Multi-brand enterprise container.
- **`BRAND`:** Brand-specific aesthetic DNA and voice.
- **`CAMPAIGN`:** Single seasonal campaign execution.
