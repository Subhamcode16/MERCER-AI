# Phase 27: Direction Management & Comparative UX

## 1. Creative Direction Cards
Each direction card provides a comprehensive strategic profile:
- **Title & Core Idea:** Concise conceptual premise.
- **Narrative Angle:** Emotional and narrative posture.
- **Target Audience Tension:** Specific psychological friction being resolved.
- **Visual DNA Tokens:** Core stylistic descriptors for prompt compilation.
- **Palette Motifs:** Exact HEX color swatches and mood tones.
- **Scores:** Confidence Score and Feasibility Score.
- **Trade-Offs & Strategic Risks:** Clear articulation of production constraints and brand risks.

## 2. Direction Selection & Refinement
Operators can iteratively refine directions by adding targeted instructions (e.g., "Deepen raking shadows on concrete textures") or select candidate directions to proceed into Visual Exploration.
