# Phase 27: Evidence Explorer & Explainability UX

## 1. Explainability Cards
Every strategic recommendation or direction card in the studio links to an `ExplainabilityCard` containing:
- **Core Rationale:** High-level summary of why the choice was synthesized.
- **Evidence Citations:** Grounded snippets from brand dossiers, trend reports, or past campaigns with confidence weights.
- **Model Attribution:** Clear declaration of the workforce departments or models involved.
- **Invariants Validated:** Confirmation of core system invariant compliance.
