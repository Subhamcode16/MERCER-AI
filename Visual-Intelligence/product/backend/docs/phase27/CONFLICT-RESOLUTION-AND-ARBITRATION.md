# Phase 27: Departmental Conflict Resolution & Arbitration

## 1. Divergence Transparency
When workforce departments reach differing conclusions (e.g. Creative Direction proposing austere monochromatic minimalism vs. Performance Marketing pushing for high-chroma neon saturation):
- The divergence is NOT hidden, averaged out, or suppressed.
- A `CreativeConflict` record is created, detailing each department's standpoint, supporting evidence, and risk analysis.

## 2. Human-in-the-Loop Arbitration
The human Creative Director reviews the comparative viewpoints and selects a resolution or synthesizes a hybrid approach.
