# Phase 27: Studio Review & Critique Engine

## 1. 5-Dimensional Review Rubric
The review engine performs automated evaluation across five critical pillars:

1. **Brand Alignment (Weight: 25%):** Compliance with brand color guidelines, typography, and silhouette rules.
2. **Aesthetic Fidelity (Weight: 20%):** Micro-contrast, fabric weave sharpness, lighting falloff, and color grading.
3. **Emotional Resonance (Weight: 20%):** Evocation of intended psychological anchors (e.g. quiet empowerment, serene majesty).
4. **Technical Execution (Weight: 20%):** Absence of generative anomalies, limb deformities, or texture clipping.
5. **Channel Suitability (Weight: 15%):** Composition framing and focal points across mobile and desktop crop safe zones.

## 2. Thresholds & Pass Criteria
- **Pass Threshold:** Overall average score $\ge 0.85$ with zero critical issues.
- Assets failing review are routed back to Visual Development with actionable critique notes and suggested parameter adjustments.
