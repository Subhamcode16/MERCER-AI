# Phase 27: Creative Intelligence Radar & Synthesis Specification

## 1. Multi-Modal Synthesis Architecture
The Creative Intelligence Radar combines three analytical pipelines:
1. **Market Signal Radar:** Real-time cultural shifts, competitor movements, and macro-aesthetic trajectories.
2. **Audience Tension Identifier:** Pinpoints deep emotional anchors and psychological frictions within the demographic.
3. **Creative Hypothesis Generator:** Formulates testable strategic narrative theses that resolve the audience tension.

## 2. Evidence Grounding & Confidence
Every market signal and hypothesis is tagged with its source attribution and a calibrated confidence score ($0.0 \le c \le 1.0$), ensuring zero hallucinations or unsubstantiated claims reach the Creative Director's desk.
