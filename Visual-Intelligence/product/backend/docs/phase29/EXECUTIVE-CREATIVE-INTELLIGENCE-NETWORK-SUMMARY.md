### 3. Key Subsystems Delivered

1. **Organizational Intelligence Graph (`graph/`):** Unified directed property graph with 22 entity types, cryptographic provenance hash edges, and strict tenant isolation partitions.
2. **Strategic Signal Engine (`signals/`):** 15 signal classes with explicit epistemic status, freshness scores, and unobserved state preservation.
3. **Hypothesis Lifecycle & 5-Scenario Foresight (`hypotheses/`, `foresight/`):** 10 lifecycle transitions and the complete 5-scenario matrix (`BASELINE`, `UPSIDE`, `DOWNSIDE`, `DISRUPTION`, `UNKNOWN`).
4. **Opportunity & Risk Intelligence (`opportunities/`, `risks/`):** Structured opportunity evaluation and severity-graded risk tracking.
5. **Strategic Recommendation Engine (`recommendations/`):** 12-stage decision contracts with automated quality downgrading for stale or conflicted evidence.
6. **Cross-Client & External Boundaries (`cross_client/`, `external/`):** 6-stage de-identification pipeline with semantic leakage scoring and prompt injection defenses.
7. **Intelligence-to-Campaign Bridge & Decision Memory (`bridge/`, `decision_memory/`):** Strict human approval gates and decoupled tracking of Decision Quality vs Subsequent Outcome Quality.
8. **Observability, Rollback & API (`observability/`, `rollback/`, `governance/`, `api/`):** Real-time observatory telemetry, instant recommendation withdrawal, and REST API routing.
