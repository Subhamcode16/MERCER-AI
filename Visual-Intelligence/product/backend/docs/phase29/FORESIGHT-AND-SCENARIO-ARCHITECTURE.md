# FORESIGHT-AND-SCENARIO-ARCHITECTURE.md
## Phase 29 Architecture Specification: Foresight & 5-Scenario Matrix

---

### 1. Overview

The **Foresight Engine** explores plausible strategic futures without collapsing multiple possibilities into a false deterministic single forecast.

Under the invariant:
$$\mathbf{Scenario \neq Forecast} \quad \mathbf{Prediction \neq Certainty}$$

---

### 2. Mandatory 5-Scenario Matrix

Every strategic foresight inquiry constructs five structured archetypes:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                             5-Scenario Matrix                                 |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. BASELINE     : Historical performance and platform variance continue.     |
| 2. UPSIDE       : Upper-quartile adoption and creative resonance accelerate.  |
| 3. DOWNSIDE     : Rapid consumer fatigue, algorithmic shifts, or CTR decay.   |
| 4. DISRUPTION   : Structural market shocks or competitive innovations.        |
| 5. UNKNOWN      : Materially unobserved states requiring experiment trials.   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

### 3. Scenario Attributes

Each generated scenario contains:
- `scenario_id` & `archetype`
- `initiating_signals` & `base_assumptions`
- `bounded_likelihood` & `uncertainty_score`
- `leading_indicators` (early warning metrics)
- `lagging_indicators` (retrospective outcome metrics)
- `potential_consequences` & `monitoring_actions`
