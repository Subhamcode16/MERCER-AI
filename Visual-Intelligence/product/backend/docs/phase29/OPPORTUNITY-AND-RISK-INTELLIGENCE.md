# OPPORTUNITY-AND-RISK-INTELLIGENCE.md
## Phase 29 Architecture Specification: Opportunity & Risk Intelligence

---

### 1. Overview

Opportunities and risks are explicitly modeled and segregated from neutral informational signals.

---

### 2. Opportunity Tiers

- `IMMEDIATE_ACTION`: Low-risk, high-evidence tactical adjustments.
- `EXPERIMENT_REQUIRED`: Promising hypothesis requiring bounded exploratory budget.
- `MONITOR_ONLY`: Early-stage trend requiring tracking before budget commitment.

---

### 3. Risk Severity & Escalation

- `LOW`: Minor performance variation within acceptable parameters.
- `MEDIUM`: Moderate fatigue or audience divergence requiring creative rotation.
- `HIGH`: Severe ad burnout or platform shift requiring strategy realignment.
- `CRITICAL`: Immediate threat to brand equity or massive budget inefficiency triggering mandatory escalation.
