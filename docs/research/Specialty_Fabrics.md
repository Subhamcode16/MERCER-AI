# 🧵 Concept: Specialty Fabrics

This note lists specialized, regional, and high-complexity fabrics, documenting their weaves, weight, and key visual attributes.

---

## 🌟 Banarasi
* **Weave:** Brocade.
* **Weight:** Heavy (due to heavy gold or silver thread/zari work).
* **Reflectivity:** Extremely high, metallic sheen.
* **Texture:** Intricate brocaded patterns.
* **Common Use Cases:** Luxurious silks, wedding wear, and high-end festive garments.

## 🌟 Kanjivaram (Kanchipuram)
* **Weave:** Traditional temple borders and silk weaves.
* **Weight:** Thick and substantial.
* **Texture:** Heavy, premium mulberry silk.
* **Characteristics:** High durability, vibrant contrasting borders.

## 🌟 Organza
* **Weave:** Sheer plain weave.
* **Stiffness:** High, retains volume and flare.
* **Transparency:** Very high, translucent fabric.
* **Common Use Cases:** Overlays, sheer panels, sleeves, and dupatta details.

## 🌟 Georgette
* **Weave:** Crepe-like crinkled weave.
* **Drape:** Soft, fluid, and flowing drape.
* **Transparency:** Semi-sheer.
* **Common Use Cases:** Flowing dresses, lightweight saris, and kurtis.
