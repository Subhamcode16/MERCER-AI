# 👕 Concept: Garment Taxonomy

This note describes the structural composition and top-to-bottom layout of traditional garments. It serves as a schema for image segmentation, tagging, and creative synthesis.

---

## 🥻 Saree (Sari)
An unstitched, elegantly draped garment.
- **Body:** The main expanse of fabric [[Silk]], [[Cotton]], [[Specialty_Fabrics]].
- **Border:** The woven/embroidered edge, often featuring contrasting colors or zari work.
- **Pallu:** The loose end draped over the shoulder, usually the most decorated section.
- **Pleats:** Folded sections tucked at the waist, providing drape volume and movement.
- **Fall:** A protective inner lining stitched to the bottom hem to add weight and prevent damage.
- **Blouse:** Tailored crop top worn underneath.
- **Tassels:** Optional decorative fringes on the end of the pallu.

---

## 👗 Kurti (Tunic)
A tailored, upper garment.
- **Neckline:** Collar or cut design (e.g. V-neck, sweetheart, Mandarin collar).
- **Sleeves:** Arm coverage (length and fit variation).
- **Hemline:** Bottom edge (straight, asymmetric, flared).
- **Length:** Vertical sizing (short, knee-length, ankle-length).
- **Fit:** Silhouette style (A-line, straight, Anarkali style).
- **Embroidery:** Embellishments or thread work.

---

## 💃 Lehenga
A traditional three-piece festive skirt ensemble.
- **Skirt (Lehenga):** Voluminous floor-length skirt.
- **Can-can:** Stiff mesh under-skirt layer to add flare and structured volume.
- ** Dupatta:** A long coordinating scarf/veil draped over the head or shoulder.
- **Blouse (Choli):** A fitted, cropped bodice.
- **Border:** Decorative borders at the hemline of the skirt and dupatta.
- **Flare:** Total lower circumference (measured in meters).
