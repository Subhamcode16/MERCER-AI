# 🧵 Concept: Silk

Silk is one of the foundational textiles in the Fashion Knowledge Wiki. It represents luxury, high drapability, and dynamic reflectivity.

---

## 🧬 Characteristics

- **Weave Options:** Plain weave, velvet, chenille, chiffon, corduroy, crepe, jersey, knit, lace, organza, plush, satin, serge, taffeta, tulle, tweed, and patterned silks on draw-looms.
- **Texture:** Very smooth and fine; Velvet variants feature a short, dense pile.
- **Embroidery Compatibility:** High compatibility with chain stitch, ari embroidery, and fine silk threads.
- **Reflectivity:** High (natural luster), especially on satin and brocade weaves.
- **Common Use Cases:** Courtly clothing, home furnishings, saris, skirts, and tailored jackets.
