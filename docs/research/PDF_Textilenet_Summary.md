---

## 🔍 Key Findings & Core Concept

- **Dataset Size:** Contains over **760,000 textile images** representing rich physical, chemical, and visual variations of materials.
- **Scientific Taxonomy:** Instead of raw tags, TextileNet uses a hierarchical, material taxonomy based on fiber chemistry, weave structures, and mechanical characteristics.
- **Applications in the Platform:**
  - Used to build the core **Product DNA** mapping logic.
  - Serves as the ground truth ontology for training the Attribute Extraction Engine.
  - Establishes a standard naming convention for raw fiber composites, weaving patterns (e.g. twill, satin, jacquard), and weights.
