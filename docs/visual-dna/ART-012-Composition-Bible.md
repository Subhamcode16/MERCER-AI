# ART-012 — Composition Bible
**Layer:** Creative Intelligence (Think)
**Agent:** `art-director`
**Movement:** I — The Threshold

---

## 1. Negative Space
Negative space is our primary material. It is not "empty space"; it is the atmosphere that gives the subject weight.
- At least 60% of the screen must remain unpopulated at all times.

## 2. Asymmetrical Balance
- We reject perfect symmetry. Perfect symmetry feels mechanical.
- If a heavy geometric mass exists on the lower left, it must be balanced by delicate, bright typography on the upper right.

## 3. The Grid
- 12-column underlying grid, but UI elements lock to the outer margins (columns 1-2 and 11-12).
- The center columns (4-9) are reserved exclusively for the 3D physical elements (The Silk, The Architecture). UI never crosses the center unless it is the final title card.

## 4. Focal Hierarchy
- **Primary:** The light reflecting off the Silk.
- **Secondary:** The primary typography (H1).
- **Tertiary:** The architectural shadows.
