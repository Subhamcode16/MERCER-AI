# ART-019 — Material Bible
**Layer:** Creative Intelligence (Think)
**Agent:** `art-director`
**Movement:** I — The Threshold

---

## 1. Core Materials
We only use physical, terrestrial materials. No plastics, no holograms, no neon.

### A. The Silk
- **Weight:** Heavy. Like theatrical curtains, not a sheer scarf.
- **Color:** Deep Obsidian (`#080808`) with Bronze/Gold (`#B08D57`) specular highlights.
- **Texture:** Fine woven normal map.

### B. The Limestone
- **Weight:** Monumental.
- **Color:** Warm Charcoal (`#1C1B1A`).
- **Texture:** Porous, slightly eroded edges.

### C. The Glass (UI Layer)
- **Refraction:** Index of Refraction (IOR) 1.5. 
- **Dispersion:** None. We want clean, elegant glass, not chromatic aberration which feels too "cyberpunk".

## 2. Forbidden Materials
- Chrome
- Glossy Plastics
- Emissive screens or neon strips
- Carbon Fiber
