## ILYREN Institutional Intelligence & Strategic Operations Layer
### Continuous Strategy, Decision Portfolio, Horizons & Governed Operationalization

**Status:** Engineering Directive

**Execution principle:** Fast, evidence-driven, fail-closed

## 1. Executive Objective

Phase 30 establishes ILYREN's **Institutional Intelligence & Strategic Operations Layer**.

Phase 29 established organizational decision intelligence:

> Governed Knowledge → Cross-Campaign Synthesis → Strategic Signal → Hypothesis → Scenario/Foresight → Recommendation → Human Decision → Experiment/Campaign → Phase 28 Learning.

Phase 30 operationalizes that intelligence continuously over time.

It provides:
- strategic horizons;
- decision portfolios;
- organizational memory;
- intelligence prioritization;
- strategic cadence;
- initiative tracking;
- strategic operating rooms;
- assumption monitoring;
- strategic drift detection;
- evidence-backed recurring strategic reviews;
- controlled autonomous preparation;
- governed bridges into campaigns, experiments, and Phase 28 learning.

**This is not another autonomous agent layer.** It is a strategic operating fabric above Phase 29.

## 5. Institutional Strategic Model

### Strategic Objective
Human-defined strategic outcome with:
- objective ID;
- owner;
- scope;
- description;
- success criteria;
- time horizon;
- priority;
- status;
- evidence basis;
- creation authority;
- review cadence.

Objectives cannot be created by model output alone.

### Strategic Horizon

Required horizons:
- `NOW`
- `NEXT`
- `LATER`
- `FUTURE`
- `UNKNOWN`

### Decision Portfolio

Each decision contains:
- decision ID;
- strategic objective;
- decision question;
- owner;
- deadline;
- evidence set;
- recommendation history;
- alternatives;
- assumptions;
- uncertainty;
- human decision;
- status;
- expected outcome;
- review date;
- actual outcome;
- decision-quality assessment.

Decision records are append-only with governed amendments.

### Strategic Initiative

Links:
- objective;
- decision;
- owner;
- authorized scope;
- expected outcomes;
- experiments;
- campaigns;
- dependencies;
- risks;
- review cadence.

An initiative does not itself authorize arbitrary execution.

### Strategic Commitment

Human-approved organizational commitment such as a launch target, milestone, experiment, partnership objective, or campaign commitment.

Every commitment requires an owner and expiration/review condition.

### Strategic Assumption

Every material strategy must expose its assumptions.

Each assumption records:
- statement;
- evidence;
- confidence;
- validation method;
- review/expiry date;
- status;
- contradiction history.

## 7. Decision Portfolio Intelligence

Continuously identify:
- decisions approaching review;
- decisions lacking evidence;
- decisions using stale evidence;
- decisions with unresolved contradictions;
- changed assumptions;
- poor subsequent outcomes;
- high-uncertainty decisions;
- dependency-blocked decisions;
- decisions needing human attention;
- decisions that may safely be deferred.

Priority model:

```text
Strategic Impact
× Urgency
× Evidence Change
× Uncertainty
× Dependency Risk
× Reversibility
```

The system prioritizes **attention**, not decisions.

Support:
- daily intelligence preparation;
- weekly strategic review;
- campaign-cycle reviews;
- monthly portfolio review;

Recurring preparation produces:
- changes since prior review;

- contradictions;

## 11. Strategic Drift Detection

Detect divergence between:
- stated objective and active initiatives;
- objective and measured outcomes;
- assumptions and new evidence;
- strategy and campaign behavior;
- planned horizon and actual execution;
- expected and observed outcomes;
- strategic narrative and contradictory evidence.

Drift is a signal requiring review. It must never silently rewrite strategy.

## 15. Decision Quality

Preserve:

**Decision Quality ≠ Outcome Quality**

### Decision Quality
Evaluate:
- evidence sufficiency;
- reasoning quality;
- alternative consideration;
- uncertainty calibration;
- assumption visibility;
- process compliance.

### Outcome Quality
Evaluate:
- observed business result;
- campaign result;
- experiment result;
- objective progress.

Neither metric overwrites the other.

## 36. Engineering Principle

> **Maximum useful strategic continuity under minimum necessary authority.**

ILYREN should continuously keep the organization informed, prepared, organized, and strategically coherent without becoming an ungoverned autonomous authority.

The system continuously prepares the next intelligent decision.

The human remains the source of consequential institutional authority.
