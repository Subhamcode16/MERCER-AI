# LAB-08: Prompt Language
**Department:** `prompt-engineer`

This lab translates Labs 1-7 into machine-readable prompt tokens for AI generation layers.

## 1. Positive Prompt Formula
**[Optics] + [Environment] + [Subject] + [Material] + [Lighting] + [Color Science]**

> "Cinematic 50mm photography, shot on ARRI Alexa 65, T2.8, shallow depth of field. A dark, brutalist architectural space made of warm charcoal limestone. At the center, a flowing, abstract sculptural form made of obsidian silk. The silk has subtle subsurface scattering, looking slightly translucent at the edges. Extreme minimalism, 60% negative space, symmetrical background. A single dramatic overhead spotlight casting hard shadows. Deep cyan shadows, warm amber highlights, high contrast, Kodak 2383 film stock, visible 35mm grain, photorealistic, 8k."

## 2. Negative Prompt Formula
**[Anti-Optics] + [Anti-Environment] + [Anti-Material] + [Anti-Lighting]**

> "UI, text, interface, wide angle, fisheye, geometric distortion, bright, daylight, flat lighting, low contrast, plastic, CGI, 3D render, glossy floor, symmetrical silk, cluttered, messy, multiple light sources, soft box, rim light, oversaturated."
