# LAB-06: Lighting Physics
**Department:** `lighting-director`

## 1. The Key Light (The Spotlight)
- **Motivation**: A single, powerful architectural spotlight mounted directly overhead.
- **Quality**: Hard light. The edges of the beam (penumbra) are sharp, creating a stark geometric shape on the floor.
- **Intensity**: High. It is the sole source of significant illumination.
- **Color**: Warm Amber (`4500K`).

## 2. Fill Light
- **Motivation**: None.
- **Rule**: There are no artificial fill lights. We embrace the darkness.

## 3. Ambient Bounce
- **Motivation**: The light from the spotlight bouncing off the limestone floor.
- **Quality**: Extremely soft, low intensity.
- **Color**: Inherits the warmth of the key light mixed with the charcoal of the floor.

## 4. Shadows
- **Quality**: Deep, crushing, high-contrast. They define the negative space.
