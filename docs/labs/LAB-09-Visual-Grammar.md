## The 5 Laws of the Visual Grammar

### I. The Law of the Lens
Everything is viewed through a 50mm lens. There is no spatial distortion. The foreground is clear. The background falls into soft bokeh. The subject is perfectly sharp.

### II. The Law of the Void
60% of the screen is negative space. The void is not empty; it is filled with 35mm grain and deep charcoal shadows. The architecture is symmetrical; the subject is not.

### III. The Law of the Single Source
There is only one light source—a hard overhead spotlight. No rim lights. No fill lights. We expose for the highlight and let the shadows crush.

### IV. The Law of the Silk
The Obsidian Silk must exhibit subsurface scattering. It is dark, but catches the light smoothly. It feels heavy but fluid.

### V. The Law of Film
We are simulating reality. Kodak 2383 print emulation. Cool shadows. Warm highlights. Smooth roll-off. No UI elements may exist in the generated images.
