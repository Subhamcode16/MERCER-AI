# LAB-07: Reference Library
**Department:** `research-curator`

## 1. Architectural References
- **Tadao Ando**: The use of pure geometry, heavy concrete/limestone, and the interaction of stark shadows against massive walls.
- **Peter Zumthor (Therme Vals)**: The atmospheric, moody integration of stone, water-like textures, and single-source lighting.

## 2. Photography References
- **Herb Ritts**: Black and white sculptural forms, high contrast, focusing on the human/fabric shape against nothingness.
- **Roger Deakins (Blade Runner 2049/Wallace Corp)**: The specific use of deep, golden overhead lighting against pitch-black architectural voids.
- **Alexander McQueen Runway Staging**: Theatrical, moody, single-spotlight reveals on complex, heavy fabrics.

## 3. Texture References
- **Obsidian Glass**: For the specular highlights.
- **Raw Silk**: For the weight and folding mechanics.
- **Brutalist Concrete**: For the environmental absorption of light.
