# LAB-01: Camera Physics
**Department:** `cinematographer`

## 1. Sensor
- **Format**: ARRI Alexa 65 (Large Format Digital).
- **Why**: Large format provides a shallower depth of field at wider angles and an expansive, immersive field of view, critical for capturing the vastness of the architectural void while keeping the subject intimate.

## 2. Emulation & Stock
- **Target Emulation**: Kodak Vision3 500T 5219.
- **Why**: Tungsten-balanced stock handles the extreme contrast of a single spotlight beautifully, maintaining rich, dense blacks without crushing detail.

## 3. Grain Structure
- **Profile**: Fine-to-medium organic 35mm grain.
- **Behavior**: The grain must be visible, particularly dancing within the deep charcoal voids of the negative space. It provides texture to silence.

## 4. Dynamic Range
- **Profile**: 14+ stops. 
- **Exposure Target**: Exposed for the highlights (the silk catching the light), allowing the architectural environment to fall deep into the toe of the exposure curve (underexposed by 2-3 stops).
