# LAB-02: Lens Physics
**Department:** `cinematographer`

## 1. Focal Length
- **Target**: 50mm Prime (e.g., ARRI Master Prime).
- **Why**: The 50mm provides zero spatial distortion. It represents the human eye, establishing a grounded, objective reality before the abstract elements are introduced.

## 2. Aperture & Depth of Field
- **T-Stop**: T2.8.
- **Falloff**: The depth of field should hold the entire volume of the Obsidian Silk in sharp focus, but the background limestone architecture should fall into a soft, creamy blur. The transition must be gradual.

## 3. Optical Artifacts
- **Halation**: Subtle red/orange halation localized *only* on the highest contrast edges where the pure spotlight strikes the silk.
- **Vignetting**: Heavy, optical vignetting at the corners, naturally driving the eye toward the center void.
- **Distortion**: Zero geometric distortion. Straight architectural lines must remain perfectly straight. No barrel distortion.
