# LAB-03: Composition
**Department:** `cinematographer`

## 1. Spatial Division
- **Negative Space**: 60% to 70% of the frame must be completely empty architectural void. 
- **Subject Scale**: The primary subject (the Silk) occupies no more than 30% of the frame.

## 2. Framing Laws
- **Center-Weighting**: The primary subject is anchored dead center.
- **Symmetry vs. Asymmetry**: The brutalist background architecture is rigidly symmetrical and grid-aligned. The foreground subject (the Silk) is entirely organic, fluid, and asymmetrical. This contrast creates the visual tension.

## 3. The Z-Axis (Depth)
- **Foreground**: Clean. No obstructing elements.
- **Midground**: The subject, perfectly sharp.
- **Background**: Infinite depth, fading off into total darkness.
