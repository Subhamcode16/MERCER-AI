# LAB-05: Material Physics
**Department:** `material-director`

## 1. Obsidian Silk (The Subject)
- **Base Color**: Extremely dark (`#0A0A0A`). It is almost black, but catches light.
- **Subsurface Scattering (Transmission)**: High. When light hits the edges or folds, the material must scatter light internally, creating a delicate, slightly translucent rim.
- **Index of Refraction (IOR)**: 1.5. Simulating physical fabric/glass hybrids.
- **Roughness**: Low (0.2). It must look smooth, heavy, and expensive, reflecting the spotlight cleanly without scattering the specular hit too widely.

## 2. Warm Charcoal Limestone (The Environment)
- **Base Color**: Dark Grey (`#111111`) with a very slight warm undertone.
- **Texture/Normals**: High frequency detail. It must look porous, heavy, and brutalist.
- **Roughness**: Very High (0.9). It absorbs light and does not reflect specular highlights. It grounds the scene.
- **Transmission**: Zero. Completely opaque.
