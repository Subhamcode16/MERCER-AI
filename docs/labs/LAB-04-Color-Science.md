# LAB-04: Color Science
**Department:** `cinematographer`

## 1. The Grade
- **Style**: Dark, moody, high-contrast, heavily desaturated.
- **LUT Profile**: Custom Kodak 2383 Print Film emulation.

## 2. Tone Mapping
- **Shadows**: Deep charcoal. Never `#000000` pure black. Shadows must be lifted slightly (around 5-8% luminance) and tinted with a subtle cool cyan.
- **Highlights**: Warm amber/gold.
- **Roll-off**: Highlights must exhibit a smooth, filmic roll-off. Nothing clips to pure white `#FFFFFF` except the absolute center of the spotlight source (if visible).

## 3. Contrast Ratio
- **Ratio**: 8:1 (High contrast). The subject is intensely illuminated against an environment that is starved of light.
