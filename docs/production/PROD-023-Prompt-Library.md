## 1. Master Style Lock (Negative Prompts)
To prevent generic AI output when generating mood boards, storyboards, or texture bases, the following negative prompts MUST always be appended:
> `--no plastic, neon, cyberpunk, holograms, glowing lines, high saturation, sharp digital edges, modern UI, sci-fi cliches, chrome, hyper-gloss`

## 2. The Silk Prompt
> "Cinematic macro photography of heavy, dense black silk fabric draping in slow motion. Warm, directional studio lighting catching bronze and gold specular highlights in the folds. Subsurface scattering. Shot on 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, 8k resolution, photorealistic, masterful chiaroscuro lighting, architectural scale."

## 3. The Architecture Prompt
> "Monolithic ancient limestone architecture emerging from dark volumetric fog. Brutalist but warm. Carved from a single massive stone. Deep shadows, subtle ambient light reflecting off porous rock surfaces. Cinematic framing, Tadao Ando inspired, timeless, majestic, silent."

## 4. The Interface Mood Prompt
> "Elegant, perfectly spaced serif typography engraved into the air above a dark, cinematic architectural space. Monocle magazine editorial design meets high-end museum curation. Minimalist, breathless, expansive negative space."
