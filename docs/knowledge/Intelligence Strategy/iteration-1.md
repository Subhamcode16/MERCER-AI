Research Question 2 — Iteration 1
---------------------------------

> **Which product categories are best suited for a Creative Intelligence Platform that generates complete marketing campaigns from a single product image?**

Step 1: Define the Selection Principle
--------------------------------------

Before listing categories, we need to define what makes a product category compatible with our platform.

A category is suitable if a single image allows us to infer enough information to reason about the product and build a compelling campaign.

From our architecture, I propose six evaluation dimensions.

DimensionDescription**Visual Information Density**How much product information can be inferred from one image?**Creative Diversity**How many unique campaign concepts can be generated?**Brand Story Potential**How strongly does branding influence purchasing decisions?**Lifestyle Context**Can the product naturally be placed into different real-world scenes?**Reasoning Dependency**How much additional information is required beyond the image? (Lower is better.)**Campaign Frequency**How often do businesses need fresh marketing campaigns?

Step 3: Initial Classification
==============================

Rather than ranking individual products immediately, let's classify them by intelligence complexity.

### Tier A — Image-Dominant Products

These products reveal most of what we need through a single image.

Examples:

*   Apparel
    
*   Jewelry
    
*   Handbags
    
*   Ceramics
    
*   Home décor
    
*   Furniture
    
*   Candles
    

These are excellent fits.

### Tier B — Image + Moderate Brand Knowledge

The image provides a strong foundation, but additional brand context improves campaigns.

Examples:

*   Beauty
    
*   Skincare
    
*   Watches
    
*   Consumer electronics
    
*   Packaged foods
    

These are good candidates.

### Tier C — Image + Significant External Knowledge

The image alone is insufficient.

Examples:

*   Perfume
    
*   Nutritional supplements
    
*   Gourmet foods
    
*   Wine
    
*   Coffee beans (origin-focused)
    

Campaign quality depends on information the image cannot reveal.

Step 4: An Emerging Pattern
===========================

This is the most interesting insight from Iteration 1.

Our platform performs best when the product itself carries most of the creative information.

That means products with:

*   distinctive materials,
    
*   visible craftsmanship,
    
*   recognizable forms,
    
*   and strong visual identity
    

are naturally aligned with the architecture we've built.

This reinforces why fashion—and especially textiles—was such a strong starting point.
