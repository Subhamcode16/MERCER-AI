=======

Iteration 1 — Designing the End-to-End User Journey
---------------------------------------------------

I don't want to design screens first.

I want to design **the user's transformation**.

Every great product transforms its user.

ProductUser, BeforeUser, AfterFigma Ideas in your head Interactive designs, Shopify products, online business, and Notion Scattered notes, organised knowledge GitHub Source code Collaborative software **Creative Intelligence Platform** Product + business goal Complete launch-ready campaign

That last line becomes our design principle.

Step 1 — Define the User's Starting Point
=========================================

When a customer opens the platform, what do they actually have?

Not a campaign.

Usually just fragments.

For example:

*   A new handbag.
    
*   A skincare bottle.
    
*   A pair of sneakers.
    
*   A jewellery collection.
    

Maybe they also have:

*   Brand guidelines.
    
*   A logo.
    
*   Product photos.
    
*   A launch deadline.
    

That's it.

Everything else still needs to be discovered.

Step 2 — Define the End State
=============================

When they leave the platform, they shouldn't just have images.

They should have confidence.

Specifically, they should leave with:

*   A campaign strategy.
    
*   Creative direction.
    
*   Marketing assets.
    
*   Creative rationale.
    
*   Multi-channel adaptations.
    
*   Everything is ready for launch.
    

The platform doesn't generate files.

It **reduces uncertainty**.

Step 3 — The Transformation Journey
===================================

Every interaction should move the user through a series of increasingly informed states.

Step 4 — The Six Core Moments
=============================

Rather than designing dozens of screens, let's identify the six moments that define the experience.

Moment 1 — Welcome
------------------

The platform asks:

> "What are we creating today?"

Not:

> "Write a prompt."

This immediately shifts the mindset from image generation to campaign creation.

Moment 2 — Product Understanding
--------------------------------

The user uploads the product.

The platform begins reasoning.

Instead of displaying technical analysis, it communicates understanding in human language.

For example:

> "This appears to be a structured leather handbag with a minimalist aesthetic suitable for premium urban consumers."

The user feels understood.

Moment 3 — Collaborative Discovery
----------------------------------

Instead of filling out a long form, the platform has a focused conversation.

Questions adapt dynamically.

Examples:

*   Who is this for?
    
*   Is this a seasonal launch?
    
*   Is the goal awareness or sales?
    
*   What feeling should customers associate with this product?
    

This is not data collection.

It is collaborative strategy building.

Moment 4 — Creative Reveal
--------------------------

This should be the platform's signature moment.

Instead of immediately showing images, it presents its thinking.

For example:

> **Campaign Direction**
> 
> Quiet Luxury for Modern Professionals
> 
> Primary Emotion: Confidence
> 
> Visual Language: Editorial Minimalism
> 
> Hero Scene: Natural morning light in an architectural setting
> 
> Supporting Story: Everyday sophistication.

Only after the user understands the strategy do visuals appear.

The platform demonstrates reasoning before execution.

Moment 5 — Campaign Evolution
-----------------------------

Campaign generation becomes interactive.

Users can modify strategic decisions.

Examples:

*   Make it more premium.
    
*   Increase emotional appeal.
    
*   Shift toward Gen Z.
    
*   Reduce visual complexity.
    
*   Emphasise craftsmanship.
    

The platform updates the reasoning first, then regenerates assets.

The user edits **intent**, not prompts.

Moment 6 — Launch Workspace
---------------------------

The final screen is not a gallery.

It is a launch center.

Everything is organised:

*   Campaign strategy
    
*   Hero assets
    
*   Social variants
    
*   Website banners
    
*   Email creatives
    
*   Creative documentation
    

The project is ready for production and publishing.

Step 5 — Emotional Journey
==========================

Most design systems map tasks.

I think we should also map emotions.

Stage User Emotion Platform Responsibility Product Upload Uncertainty Build confidence, product understanding, and curiosity. Demonstrate intelligence: Strategy Discussion, Exploration Collaborate, don't interrogateCreative Reveal: Excitement and Surprise with thoughtful reasoningCampaign RefinementControlKeep edits simple and meaningfulLaunch Confidence Deliver clarity and readiness

This emotional arc is just as important as the functional flow.

Step 6 — A New Design Principle
===============================

I think we've uncovered something that should influence every feature.

> **The platform should expose decisions before it exposes outputs.**

Today's AI products reveal results first.

Users then reverse-engineer why those results appeared.

Our platform should do the opposite.

It should explain:

*   What it believes.
    
*   Why it believes it.
    
*   What it plans to create.
    

Then it creates.

That builds trust.

The Biggest Discovery
=====================

I think we've been thinking of the product as a campaign generator.

It's actually something different.

It's a **guided creative decision system**.

Images are only one artefact produced by that system.

The real product is the sequence of decisions that leads to those images.
