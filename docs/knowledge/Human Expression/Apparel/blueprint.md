# Domain Blueprint: Apparel

## 1. Domain Definition
This module covers the cultural, material, and structural intelligence required to generate authentic marketing campaigns for general contemporary and western apparel. This includes structured garments (denim jackets, tailored suits), lightweight contemporary wear (dresses, t-shirts, activewear), and specialized tailoring, but excludes traditional ethnic wear (such as Sarees or Lehengas) which reside in their own specialized sub-modules.

## 2. Core Ontologies
- **Materials**: 
  - *Denim*: Heavy weight (12-15 oz), indigo twill weave, high stiffness.
  - *Linen*: Light weight, coarse flax texture, extremely high creasing, diffuse matte.
  - *Wool/Tweed*: Heavy weight, high fuzz texture, high insulation weight.
  - *Knit/Cotton*: Medium weight, flat loop texture, relaxed drape.
  - *Spandex/Activewear*: High elasticity, zero wrinkles, wraps body contours tightly.
- **Silhouettes**: Relaxed Fit, Slim Fit, Tailored/Structured, Oversized, Cascading Drape.
- **Seams & Construction Details**: Flatlock stitching, French seams, Topstitching, Ribbed collars, Cuffs, Hemlines.
- **Occasions**: High Fashion Editorial, Corporate Workwear, Casual Streetwear, Active Outdoor.
- **Poses & Dynamics**: Editorial walking stride, static structural lean, relaxed sitting, active running.

## 3. Reasoned Attributes
Attributes visually inferred or extracted from evidence within this domain:
- **Drape Rigidity Index (DRI)**: Scale of 0 (completely fluid, like chiffon) to 1 (extremely rigid, like heavy raw denim or tailored suits).
- **Crease Profile**: High creasing/wrinkling (Linen, Cotton) vs. low/zero creasing (Polyester, Spandex).
- **Joint stress points**: Wrinkle propagation density around elbows, knees, waist, and armpits.
- **Fit Index**: Tight/Skin-tight (contour wrapping) vs. Oversized (hanging off shoulder/seams).

## 4. Evaluation Criteria
- **Physical Drape Accuracy**: Fabric folds must respect the weight and rigidity index (e.g., linen should show sharp, organic creases at the elbows, while heavy wool hangs with massive, soft-rolling folds).
- **Construction Realism**: The rendering must display clear seams, hems, and stitching lines to prevent "AI plastic smoothness" or seamless rendering errors.
- **Pose-Fit Coordination**: The garment silhouette must physically respond to the body stance (e.g., walking shifts the fabric hem, running creates directional wind-drape).
