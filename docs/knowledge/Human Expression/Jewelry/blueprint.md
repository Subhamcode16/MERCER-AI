# Domain Blueprint: Jewelry

## 1. Domain Definition
This module covers the optical, structural, and luxury context intelligence required to generate authentic campaigns for high-end jewelry and fine watches. This includes necklaces, earrings, rings, bracelets, and precious timepieces. It excludes cheap costume jewelry or hair accessories, focusing strictly on precious metals and gemstones.

## 2. Core Ontologies
- **Precious Metals**:
  - *Polished Gold (Yellow/Rose)*: High gloss, mirror-like specular reflection, warm color tint.
  - *Platinum / White Gold*: Extreme reflectivity, neutral specular tint, high reflection mapping requirement.
  - *Brushed Finishes*: Diffuse reflection, satin sheen.
- **Gemstone Optics**:
  - *Diamond*: Extreme refractive index (2.42), high dispersion (fire/rainbow split highlights), sharp facets.
  - *Colored Gems (Emerald, Sapphire, Ruby)*: Internal light transmission, color absorption, subsurface refraction.
  - *Pearl*: Lustrous nacre finish, soft iridescent reflections.
- **Settings & Structure**: Prong setting, Bezel setting, Pavé diamonds, Channel setting.
- **Lighting Profiles**:
  - *Reflection Mapping*: Environmental reflections on metal surfaces (soft light boxes, dark studio frames).
  - *Fresnel Spots*: Hard spot key lights to hit gemstone facets and generate "sparkle" or fire.
- **Camera & Lens Rules**: 90mm-105mm macro lens, razor-thin depth-of-field, lens breathing.

## 3. Reasoned Attributes
Attributes visually inferred or extracted from evidence within this domain:
- **Refractive Index (RI)**: Optical density scale dictating how light bends inside gemstones.
- **Dispersion Index (Fire)**: Level of rainbow-like color splitting in specular reflections.
- **Reflection Map Quality**: Level of environment detail reflected in polished metal surfaces.
- **Prong Alignment**: Structural symmetry of micro-prongs holding stones in place.

## 4. Evaluation Criteria
- **Refraction & Fire Accuracy**: Gemstones must display sharp facets, internal reflections, and fire (dispersion colors) rather than appearing as flat colored glass.
- **Specular Reflection Quality**: Polished metal surfaces must reflect environment shapes (e.g., studio panels, window frames) rather than having blown-out solid white patches.
- **Prong & Detail Resolution**: Claws, prongs, and settings must be crisp and distinct without merging or warping.
- **Macro Depth-of-Field**: Backgrounds must collapse into high-end creamy bokeh, leaving only the primary jewelry piece sharp.
