# Domain Blueprint: Sarees

## 1. Domain Definition
This module covers the cultural, material, and aesthetic intelligence required to generate authentic marketing campaigns for Indian Sarees. It includes understanding of drapes, regional weaves, occasions, and styling, but excludes western formal wear or fusion garments unless explicitly stated.

## 2. Core Ontologies
- Fabric & Material (e.g., Silk, Cotton, Organza)
- Regional Weaves (e.g., Kanjeevaram, Banarasi, Chanderi)
- Occasions (e.g., Bridal, Festive, Casual, Formal)
- Styling & Draping (e.g., Nivi, Bengali, Seedha Pallu)
- Motifs & Patterns (e.g., Paisley, Zari, Temple borders)

## 3. Reasoned Attributes
Attributes visually inferred or extracted from evidence within this domain:
- Formality level
- Geographic origin/influence
- Time of day suitability (e.g., day vs. night events)
- Aesthetic mood (e.g., Royal, Minimalist, Traditional)

## 4. Evaluation Criteria
A successful campaign in this module respects the cultural weight of the garment, accurately pairs lighting and background to the material (e.g., avoiding flat lighting on reflective silk), and targets the correct consumer demographic based on occasion.
