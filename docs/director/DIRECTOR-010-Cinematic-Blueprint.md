# DIRECTOR-010 — Cinematic Blueprint
**Layer:** Creative Intelligence (Think)
**Agent:** `cinematic-director`
**Movement:** I — The Threshold

---

## 1. Sequence Mapping
The sequence is linear and scroll-linked. It cannot be skipped.
1. **The Void**: Pure atmosphere. Volumetric fog. 
2. **The Emergence**: The Silk enters the frame from the Z-axis, moving toward the camera.
3. **The Architecture**: Limestone monolithic structures fade into the background.
4. **The Interface**: Typography fades in slowly over the geometry.

## 2. Lens Language
- **Focal Length**: 50mm (mimics the human eye, intimate, distortion-free).
- **Depth of Field**: Shallow during The Emergence. Deep focus during The Architecture.
- **Aperture**: f/2.8 transitioning to f/8.

## 3. Shot Types
- **Opening**: Extreme Wide Shot (EWS). Vast negative space.
- **Middle**: Medium Close Up (MCU) on the Silk fabric folding.
- **Ending**: Wide Shot (WS) encompassing the architecture and UI.

## 4. Pacing
- The camera never stops moving entirely; a micro-drift (0.01 units/sec) must always be present to prevent static lifelessness.
- Fast cuts are strictly forbidden.
