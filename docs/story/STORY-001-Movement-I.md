# STORYBOARD-001 — Movement I: The Threshold

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked  
**Movement:** I — The Threshold  
**Duration:** 35–45 Seconds  
**Purpose:** Visual Blueprint

---

# Overview

This document is not an animation timeline.

It is the visual language of Movement I.

Every frame should feel like it belongs to the same uninterrupted cinematic sequence.

The objective is not to create six scenes.

The objective is to create one environment that gradually reveals itself.

Every shot should be capable of existing as a still photograph.

If paused anywhere, it should resemble a luxury editorial campaign rather than a website.

# Composition Rules

Every shot follows the same principles.

## Rule 01

One focal point.

---

## Rule 02

Negative space occupies at least 45%.

---

## Rule 03

Human remains secondary.

Architecture is primary.

---

## Rule 04

No decorative objects.

Everything must justify its existence.

---

## Rule 05

No visible UI until Shot 06.

---

# Motion Continuity

Every shot must inherit movement from the previous one.

Examples

Wind continues.

Light continues.

Camera never resets.

The visitor should never feel a cut.
