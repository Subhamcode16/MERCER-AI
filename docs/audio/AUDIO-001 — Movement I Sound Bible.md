# Philosophy

Sound is not decoration.

Sound is architecture.

The visitor should never consciously notice the soundtrack.

Instead, they should feel the space.

The world should sound like a real institution.

Not software.

Not science fiction.

No cinematic trailer music.

No interface beeps.

No AI sound effects.

The building itself becomes the instrument.

# Audio Identity

Imagine standing inside an empty cathedral before sunrise.

You hear:

- distant air movement
- stone absorbing sound
- subtle reverberation
- fabric responding to space

That is our starting point.

# Core Audio Principles

## Principle 01

Silence is a material.

---

## Principle 02

---

## Principle 03

The environment always speaks before the interface.

---

## Principle 04

---

# Acceptance Criteria

Movement I audio is approved when:

- Visitors cannot identify where the soundtrack begins.
- Silence feels intentional.
- The building feels alive.
- Every sound appears to originate from the physical environment.
- Removing the audio noticeably weakens the emotional impact.
- No sound draws attention to itself.
