# 🧵 Concept: Cotton

Cotton represents a highly versatile, breathable textile with wide-ranging weights (from luxury muslins to everyday utilitarian fabrics).

---

## 🧬 Characteristics

- **Weave Options:** Plain weave (Khadi), canvas, chambray, chenille, chiffon, corduroy, denim, flannel, gingham, jersey, knit, lace, lawn, plush, tulle, twill, and velvet.
- **Weight Range:** Extremely broad; ranges from ultra-lightweight (muslins) to extremely heavy/rough (canvas/denim).
- **Texture:** Varies dynamically from extremely fine/smooth to highly coarse/rough.
- **Embroidery Compatibility:** High compatibility with painted dyes, block printing, ink work, and gold paint.
- **Common Use Cases:** Dress fabrics, home furnishings, tents, everyday utilitarian clothing, sail-cloth, accessories, chintzes, and handkerchiefs.
- **Premium vs. Budget Variants:** Luxury muslins and chintzes versus inexpensive everyday handkerchiefs, ginghams, and calicos.
