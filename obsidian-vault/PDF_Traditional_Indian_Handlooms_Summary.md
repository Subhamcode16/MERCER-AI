## 🔍 Key Insights & Cultural Heritage

- **Weaving Legacy:** Details the regional variations, hand-spinning traditions (like Khadi), and traditional draw-loom (Jaala) setups.
- **Geographic Clusters:**
  - **North India:** Banarasi brocades (Zari threadwork, metallic gold/silver leaf details).
  - **South India:** Kanjivaram mulberry silk weaving, temple border patterns.
  - **East & West India:** Block-printed handlooms, indigo dying, and fine muslin weaves.
- **Platform Applications:**
  - Standardizes labeling for regional, artisanal textiles.
  - Dictates structural rules for heavy embroidery compatibility (e.g. ari embroidery vs. zari borders).
  - Provides a deep cultural-historical context used for generating dynamic marketing campaigns and regional textile descriptions.
