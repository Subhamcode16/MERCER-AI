# Domain Blueprint: Footwear

## 1. Domain Definition
This module covers the physical, material, and contextual intelligence required to generate authentic campaigns for footwear. This includes athletic sneakers, luxury leather boots, formal dress shoes, and casual sandals. It excludes structural orthopedic footwear or socks, unless explicitly required as a styling prop.

## 2. Core Ontologies
- **Footwear Construction (Parts)**:
  - *Upper*: Vamp, Tongue, Eyelets, Laces (symmetrical patterns, metal aglets).
  - *Sole*: Midsole, Outsole (tread patterns, grooved grips), Insole.
  - *Heel*: Block heel, Stiletto, Flat, Wedge.
- **Materials & Textures**:
  - *Polished Leather*: High gloss, narrow specular width, anisotropic light response.
  - *Suede*: Matte pile finish, pile-directional color shifting (like velvet).
  - *Knit Mesh*: High micro-texture, porous structure, low specular reflection.
  - *Vulcanized Rubber*: Flat semi-gloss, absorbs light on grips.
- **Camera Angles & Framing**: Low-angle hero, 3/4 side profile, flat overhead layout, macro texture detail.
- **Stances & States**: Mid-stride walk, heel pivot, running, flat static, suspended/floating.
- **Environments**: Clean studio cyclorama, wet city street asphalt, rugged outdoor stone, gym floor wood.

## 3. Reasoned Attributes
Attributes visually inferred or extracted from evidence within this domain:
- **Grounding Index**: Absolute alignment of the shoe sole to the ground plane, accompanied by realistic contact shadows.
- **Lacing Symmetry**: Symmetrical eyelet alignment and physics-compliant lace drop.
- **Tread Resolution**: Clarity of deep sole treads and grips without muddy rendering errors.
- **Formality Score**: Technical athletic (mesh/rubber) vs. classic formal (polished leather/stiff sole).

## 4. Evaluation Criteria
- **Absolute Grounding (No Hovering)**: The shoe must sit firmly on the ground plane. Contact shadows must be dense directly under the sole and diffuse outward, avoiding floating effects common in AI generations.
- **Lace & Eyelet Integrity**: Symmetrical eyelets and physically accurate laces (not blending into the upper).
- **Material Authenticity**: Suede must show grain pile variation, and leather must display realistic specular light reflection at curves.
