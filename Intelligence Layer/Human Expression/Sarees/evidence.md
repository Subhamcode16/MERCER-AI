> zari_properties: "Metallic polyester or gold/silver wrapped thread, high-gloss metallic, isotropic catchlights"

- **Claim 1 (Physics):** Banarasi silk does not billow or float; it forms heavy, structured, large, crisp folds. -> Promoted to KU-SAREE-001

Properties apply to heavy brocades (300-700 GSM). Lighter modern blends may show slightly more fluid draping.
