# My recommendation for the next 90 days

If I were setting engineering priorities, they would be:

1. **Build the end-to-end MVP** for a single vertical: *single saree → complete campaign package*. Don't add new intelligence during this phase unless validation clearly exposes a gap.
2. **Run expert evaluations** with textile merchants, photographers, designers, and marketers. Record not just scores but *why* they preferred or rejected outputs.
3. **Implement the feedback flywheel** so every approval, rejection, and edit becomes structured learning for the platform.
4. **Measure business metrics** like approval rate, edit time, campaign consistency, and customer satisfaction—not just image quality.
5. **Only after the MVP consistently outperforms prompt-only workflows** should you invest in expanding the ontology to more garment categories.

## The milestone I'd now optimize for isn't another architecture document.

It's this sentence:

> **"A textile brand uploaded one product photo and published an entire campaign generated by the platform with minimal manual editing."**

If you can repeatedly achieve that outcome with measurable improvements over existing workflows, you'll have validated not just the technology, but the business value of the Creative Intelligence Infrastructure you've spent all this time designing. That's the transition from a research platform to a product company.
