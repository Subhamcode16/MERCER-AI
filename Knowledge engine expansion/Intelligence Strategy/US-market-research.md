# RES-002
# US Market Research
## Question 2 — Which Intelligence Domains should the Creative Intelligence Platform support?

**Status:** Locked  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Define the long-term intelligence expansion strategy of the Creative Intelligence Platform and identify the optimal launch domain for the US market.

---

# Research Question

> **Which intelligence domains are best suited for a Creative Intelligence Platform that generates complete marketing campaigns from a single product image?**

---

# Executive Summary

The platform should **not** expand product-by-product.

Instead, it should expand **intelligence domain by intelligence domain.**

A product category is simply an instance of a larger creative knowledge domain.

The platform's architecture should therefore organize intelligence around **shared reasoning capabilities**, not individual products.

---

# Core Discovery

The original expansion strategy was:

```
Sarees

↓

Dresses

↓

T-Shirts

↓

Shoes

↓

Jewelry
```

This approach was rejected.

Products do not define intelligence.

Knowledge domains define intelligence.

The platform should instead evolve as:

```
Creative Intelligence Platform

↓

Human Expression Intelligence

↓

Living Environment Intelligence

↓

Personal Care Intelligence

↓

Consumption Intelligence

↓

Technology Intelligence

↓

Universal Creative Intelligence
```

---

# Architectural Principle

The platform should not ask:

> "Which product is this?"

Instead it should ask:

> "Which intelligence domain does this belong to?"

Once the domain is identified, domain-specific reasoning becomes available.

This creates a scalable and extensible architecture.

---

# Intelligence Evaluation Framework

Every potential domain was evaluated using six dimensions.

| Evaluation Criteria | Description |
|--------------------|-------------|
| Visual Information Density | How much information can be inferred from one product image? |
| Creative Diversity | Number of campaign concepts that can be generated |
| Brand Story Potential | Importance of storytelling within the category |
| Lifestyle Context | Ability to place products naturally into multiple environments |
| Reasoning Simplicity | Amount of external knowledge required beyond the image |
| Campaign Frequency | Frequency with which brands require new campaigns |

---

# Market & Technical Findings

The strongest intelligence domains share one characteristic:

> **Most of the creative reasoning can be derived directly from visual information.**

As hidden product attributes increase, dependence on external knowledge also increases.

This reduces campaign quality from a single-image workflow.

# Strategic Insight

The company should organize intelligence around **knowledge domains**, not products.

Each domain represents its own ecosystem of:

- Ontologies
- Knowledge Graphs
- Creative Rules
- Campaign Patterns
- Material Understanding
- Visual Grammar
- Evaluation Metrics
- Domain Memory

This aligns directly with the platform's modular architecture.

---

# Commercial Insight

The strongest commercial opportunities occur where three conditions overlap:

- Products are visually expressive.
- Brands generate campaigns frequently.
- Creative storytelling strongly influences purchasing decisions.

These conditions are best satisfied by visual consumer brands.

# Final Decisions

The following decisions are officially locked.

### Decision 1

Expand by **Intelligence Domains**, not individual product categories.

---

### Decision 2

The first production domain is:

> **Human Expression Intelligence**

---

### Decision 3

The current Saree Intelligence becomes the first specialization within Human Expression Intelligence rather than the identity of the platform.

---

### Decision 4

Each Intelligence Domain owns its own:

- Ontology
- Knowledge Graph
- Creative Rules
- Visual Grammar
- Campaign Patterns
- Domain Memory
- Evaluation Framework

while sharing the platform's universal reasoning infrastructure.

---

### Decision 5

The long-term objective is not to support every product.

The long-term objective is to understand every **visual consumer domain** through specialized creative intelligence built on a common reasoning platform.
