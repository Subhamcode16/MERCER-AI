# FABRIC & STYLING DATABASE (FDB-001)

---

## 1. Fabric Texture Nodes

### FABRIC-001: Heavy Banarasi Brocade
- **Material properties:** Stiff, heavy, highly reflective metallic threads (Zari) woven into silk.
- **Drape behavior:** Falls in wide, structural folds rather than clinging. Does not flow easily in wind.
- **Lighting response:** Catches directional light intensely on the metallic threads, creating micro-contrast against the silk base.
- **Prompt language:** "heavy Banarasi silk brocade, stiff structural drape, rich Zari metallic thread embroidery catching the light"

### FABRIC-002: Sheer Net / Tulle (Dupatta)
- **Material properties:** Transparent, lightweight, stiff but airy.
- **Drape behavior:** Floats easily, holds volume when bunched, creates a veil effect over the face or body.
- **Lighting response:** Diffuses backlight beautifully, creating a glowing ethereal effect (pairs well with Ethereal Backlight).
- **Prompt language:** "sheer net dupatta, lightweight translucent fabric, glowing softly against backlight"

## 2. Bridal Ornament Nodes

### ORNAMENT-001: The Nath (Nose Ring)
- **Placement:** Pierced or clipped to the left nostril, often with a chain extending to the ear.
- **Material:** Gold, pearls, Kundan stones.
- **Visual Impact:** Creates a strong focal point on the face, heavily framing the cheek and jawline. Crucial for traditional bridal aesthetics.

### ORNAMENT-002: Maang Tikka / Matha Patti
- **Placement:** Resting on the center parting of the hair, sometimes extending along the hairline.
- **Material:** Gold, pearls, Kundan, Polki.
- **Visual Impact:** Frames the forehead and anchors the upper portion of the face, drawing attention to the eyes.
