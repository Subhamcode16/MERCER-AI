# Evidence Record: Heavy Denim Physics and Seams

## 1. Source Details
Compiled from textile engineering standards for heavy-weight denim (12-15 oz) and casual apparel manufacturing guidelines.

## 2. Raw Data / Quote
> "Denim is a rugged, stiff cotton twill fabric with low drape compliance. Folds are wide, rigid, and hold structured shapes under gravity. The indigo dyeing process leaves warp yarns dyed and weft yarns white, resulting in a dual-tone micro-texture. Double-needle orange/yellow contrast stitching runs along outer seams, pockets, and waistbands, holding raw edges flat."

## 3. Extracted Claims
- **Claim 1 (Physics):** Denim drape is stiff and holds wide, rigid folds rather than soft, cascading pleats. -> Promoted to KU-APP-001
- **Claim 2 (Construction):** Denim requires visible double-needle contrast stitching on seams and hems to read as authentic. -> Promoted to KU-APP-002

## 4. Context & Limitations
Properties apply to raw or heavy-weight denim. Lightweight stretch denim blends (with elastane) display higher drape compliance.

# Evidence Record: Linen Creasing and Surface Matte

## 1. Source Details
Extracted from interviews with editorial apparel photographers and linen brand style guides.

## 2. Raw Data / Quote
> "Linen has zero luster; it is fully diffuse and absorbs light evenly. Its defining characteristic is the organic crease pattern. Upon wear, wrinkles immediately propagate and lock at joint stress areas (inside elbows, lapels, waist). Ironed linen displays soft, rounded rolling folds, but within minutes of movement, it registers sharp, high-contrast crease lines. Attempting to render linen without creases destroys the natural look, making it resemble synthetic polyester."

## 3. Extracted Claims
- **Claim 1 (Optics):** Linen has a fully diffuse matte surface with zero sheen. -> Promoted to KU-APP-003
- **Claim 2 (Physics):** Linen immediately forms and retains sharp, organic crease lines at joints and stress points upon movement. -> Promoted to KU-APP-004

## 4. Context & Limitations
Linen blends (cotton-linen or viscose-linen) will reduce the crease retention index, resulting in softer, less defined wrinkles.

# Evidence Record: Spandex activewear Contour Wrapping

## 1. Source Details
Activewear design standards and digital rendering guidelines for high-performance athletic apparel.

## 2. Raw Data / Quote
> "Spandex activewear contains high concentrations of elastane (10-25%), generating strong elastic recovery. It wraps body contours tightly, eliminating loose hanging folds. Under joint tension, the fabric stretches smoothly without creasing, showing flatlock seams (flat, interlocking threads that sit flush with the fabric) designed to prevent chafing."

## 3. Extracted Claims
- **Claim 1 (Physics):** Spandex activewear wraps body contours tightly under elastic tension, eliminating loose folds and wrinkling at joints. -> Promoted to KU-APP-005
- **Claim 2 (Construction):** Activewear requires flat, interlocking flatlock seams running along panels instead of raised double-stitching. -> Promoted to KU-APP-006

## 4. Context & Limitations
Applies strictly to compression and high-stretch synthetic activewear.

# Evidence Record: Tailored Blazer Structure & Padding

## 1. Source Details
Savile Row tailoring structural guidelines and luxury catalog direction.

## 2. Raw Data / Quote
> "A tailored suit blazer is defined by its canvas lining and padded shoulders. The shoulder line must remain sharp and straight, projecting outward from the neck without sagging or drooping. The lapel rolls smoothly down to the button closure, creating a crisp V-shape. Flat front-lighting washes out the lapel edge shadow, making the chest read as a flat surface."

## 3. Extracted Claims
- **Claim 1 (Physics):** Tailored outerwear features structured, straight shoulder lines supported by internal padding, preventing drooping or sagging. -> Promoted to KU-APP-007
- **Claim 2 (Lighting):** Blazer lapels require directional side lighting to cast a subtle drop shadow on the chest, emphasizing tailoring depth. -> Promoted to KU-APP-008

## 4. Context & Limitations
Applies to structured formal tailoring. Unstructured or knit blazers will drape softly, conforming to natural shoulder slope.
