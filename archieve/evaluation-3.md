## If I were rebuilding this from today...

I would make one radical change.

I would **stop thinking about it as a landing page entirely**.

I'd think of it as **the onboarding sequence for the product**.

Every scroll should teach the user one capability:

1. **I understand your material.**
2. **I understand your brand.**
3. **I reason like a creative director.**
4. **I generate campaigns from those decisions.**
5. **Now you take over and art direct the result.**

If the experience teaches those five ideas visually, you'll have something that's not just beautiful—it will be memorable, distinctive, and much harder for competitors to imitate. Right now, you're about **80% of the way there**, but the remaining 20% is about communicating **intelligence**, not adding more visual richness.
