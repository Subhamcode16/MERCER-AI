## My recommendation

I would **stop designing the landing page as independent sections**.

Instead, let's create **Landing Experience v1.0**, where the entire page is one uninterrupted cinematic sequence.

The biggest rule would be:

> **There are no sections. There are only scenes.**

That single change will influence every transition, camera move, lighting cue, and interaction. It also aligns perfectly with the Creative Constitution we've been developing: one environment, one camera, many states. I think that's the conceptual leap the project needs before we continue building individual pieces.
