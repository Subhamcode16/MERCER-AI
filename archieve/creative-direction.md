## What I would tell the developer

> **Freeze the implementation.**
>
> Do not spend another week polishing shaders, particles, or transitions.
>
> The next improvements will not come from code—they will come from storytelling, cinematography, and visual direction.
>
> The engine you've built is now good enough to support a much stronger narrative. From this point onward, every animation, camera move, and transition should exist to advance the story rather than simply decorate the page.

---

## What I think we should do next

This is where I get excited.

I think we're done designing *sections*.

Now we should design the landing page exactly like a short film.

Not 4 acts.

Not 8 sections.

But **40–60 cinematic storyboard frames**.

Each frame will define:

* Camera position and movement.
* Composition.
* Material state.
* Lighting.
* Typography.
* AI behavior.
* Transition into the next frame.

Once those frames are locked, implementation becomes an exercise in execution rather than experimentation. In my opinion, that's the process that will get you from a strong prototype to something that genuinely feels worthy of an Awwwards Site of the Day.
